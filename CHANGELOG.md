# Changelog

All notable changes to Home 3D.

## Unreleased

- Home 3D reads as its own project: the last upstream names and German leftovers are gone from code, tests and docs. German keyword lists, voice phrases and Assist sentences are removed (Assist and the locating answers speak English); German stays a language pack like any other (#399).

- Editor: outdoor areas and pools copy and paste like items. Ctrl+C / Ctrl+V and Copy / Paste (in the area's panel, its floating toolbar and its right-click menu) work for an area alone or chosen together with items (Shift + click, a frame, Select several); a mixed selection also drags and deletes together. Ctrl+V puts the copy at the pointer when it is on the plan, else a step off the original (or right above it on another floor), chooses it and is one undo step; it works across floors and homes in the same browser session. A pasted area gets a new id and no Home Assistant area; a pasted pool keeps its entities and ports but not its pipes. Ctrl+D duplicates an area (#400).
- Editor: outdoor areas are easy to find. The floor page lists every outdoor area of the home (name or kind, size or a fence's length, floor) like its items list: a tap selects it, switching floor if needed, and brings it into view; it can be hidden on the plan or deleted there. The Outside panel lists them too, under "On the plan", narrowed by its search. From an upper floor the garden of the floors below shows faintly on the plan, and a tap on it offers to go there. Paths, fences, hedges and anything under a metre across can be picked from a wider margin round their outline. Fit takes the garden in while the outdoor tools, the Outside panel or the list are open (#401).
- Editor: Build and Outside open a panel like the furniture library instead of a drop-down menu with chips: a card with a small picture (drawn in code) and a plain name for each thing to draw (rectangle room, free-form room, room by measurements, wall, door or window, floor opening, roof; lawn, terrace, path, driveway, flower bed, wild patch, hedge, fence, pergola, balcony, four pool shapes and, where pipe rules are on, pool pipes). A search, the last used cards first, one "Draw areas as Rectangle / Free form" choice for outdoor areas, and a line under the cards that says how to draw the card in use or under the pointer. Keyboard (arrows, Home, End, Enter, Escape) and screen readers work as in the rest of the editor; on a phone the panel is a sheet above the bottom bar. The panel is a small lazy chunk of the editor, loaded once the editor has drawn (#402).
- Vehicles: the car colours in the item panel now repaint the Nissan Leaf and its colourways, the bicycles, mopeds, motorbikes and scooters too (16 items had their body in fixed colours). Their bodies are in the paint role, with the colourway's colour as the item's own paint (a new optional item field, `colors`), so each looks as before until repainted, and the reset goes back to it; the two-tone Leaf keeps its black roof. Garden & outdoor pack release 7: a placed vehicle changes after "Update item", which keeps the colour picked for it. A test checks that every vehicle has its body in the paint role (#404).
- Every colour field in the editor, the item editor, Settings, the View panel and the card editor is one shared field: the swatch, the colour as a hex value (#1e6ebc) to read, type or paste (#rgb, #rrggbb, rrggbb and rgb(r, g, b) are taken; anything else shows a short error and keeps the colour), and a copy button; the value is selected on focus. One change from the swatch or the text is one undo step (#403).
- Home 3D has its own icon: a snow globe holding a lit house, drawn for this project. It replaces the icon that came from the original project. Home Assistant shows it in the integrations list and the Marketplace, with a separate version for dark cards (`dark_icon.png`). The artwork is in `docs/brand/`.
- Editor: a short side panel (a free wall, a fence, a lawn, a pet) starts at the top again. The navigation row took the free height of the panel, so the back link and the form sat about 280 px down (#406).
- Editor item panel: folds remember less. Only Appearance is remembered per kind of item; Size, the glow, Screen pictures, Energy, Taps and visibility and More options open for the chosen item only, so the next item starts at its short first view again. Older remembered folds other than Appearance are left out (#407).
- Editor: one panel header for every side panel. Rooms, doors and windows, free walls, outdoor areas and pools, roof sections and roof windows, solar fields, device pins, pets and the floor settings now have the item's header: the name, "kind · place", the live state in Home Assistant's own words and in sentence case, the same lock icon button ("Lock position", or "Lock floor plan" for the parts of the plan) and a "⋯" menu. Duplicate, Delete and the kind's actions (Furnish, Place spots, Swap sides, the dormers, Fill face, To room centre, Move up and down, Close gaps) moved into "⋯"; the button rows at the bottom and the "🔓 Fix" buttons are gone. The roof and solar forms go back by the small crumb row like the rest. A pet can be deleted from its panel (#409).

## Home 3D 1.1.0 (6 November 2026)

- Time travel: items with a "When it shows" rule follow the past. A car that only shows while its tracker is home is hidden at a moment it was away, and an item with days or times shows by the replayed moment, not the clock now. The people and trackers a rule follows are replayed as home or away only (no zone, coordinates or other attributes); every other person and tracker stays out (#394).
- Editor plan: a small toolbar floats over the selected item and follows it: turn left or right by 90°, duplicate, lock position, delete, and "⋯" with the right-click menu (copy, mirror, wall view). It flips below the item near the top edge, works by keyboard (Tab from the item, arrows, Escape) and is 44 px on touch. "Select several" moved to the main toolbar. While an item is dragged the side panel is no longer drawn again on every move (#398).
- Editor plan: the selected outdoor area or flat roof outline has a "+" in the middle of every edge (as rooms have): press it to add a point, or drag it. Right-click or long-press a point for "Remove point", or choose it and press Delete; an outline keeps at least 3 points. Points take the keyboard (Tab, arrows, Delete), and dragging a room's "+" now moves the new corner instead of the next one (#395).
- Editor item panel by item kind (option A of the run 9 designs): the header names the item with its kind and room, shows its live state ("Charging 3.6 kW · 78 %"), Lock position and a "⋯" menu (Rename, Duplicate, Copy, Wall view, Delete); Position is always open with turn buttons and the hint "Drag it on the plan to move it. Drag the dot to turn it."; then the kind's main section and closed folds (Appearance, Size, Occupancy sensor or Glow, Screen pictures, Energy, Taps and visibility, More options) that show what is set and an "N set" badge, remembered per kind. A car (any vehicle item) now gets the car form: one picker and a list of what was found, with "Change what was found" for the role pickers; Mirror, Second state, consumables and Ask before switching are gone from cars, Second state shows only on beds, and the double-count warning only when the home has a circuit monitor. The car went from 55 controls and 2,912 px to 17 and 884 px, the sofa from 39 to 19. The bottom Rotate, Duplicate, Delete and Select several block is gone from the item panel (#397).
- Editor item panel, quick wins from the run 9 review: one slim navigation row ("‹ room or floor" and a fold icon) instead of two full buttons; entity pickers are named after their label for screen readers and show the friendly name with a live value ("Car battery · 78 %"), the raw entity id on hover; the colour preset selects have names; help opens on demand with an "i" button (click, tap or keyboard) instead of tooltips; only the header stays pinned while the panel scrolls; the phone sheet clears the bottom bar; plain labels ("Glow when this is on", "Fuse circuit", "Counts as on above", "Other names", "Show one more reading", "Turn back into a plain pin"). The header with Lock position, "⋯" and the turn buttons comes with the folds (#396).
- Editor with 3D beside on: a lamp or sensor that changes no longer relights the whole floor and re-syncs the 3D view several times a second. Only the rooms whose lights changed are relit (their wall shadows are kept), the 3D beside takes state changes at most once a second, and a sync that changes nothing no longer updates the view a second time. The speed check measures this on a ~330-item floor (#389).
- Editor with 3D beside on: a power or energy reading no longer rebuilds the 3D scene (the editor's plan was handed to the 3D view again on every sync), every new Home Assistant state reaches the 3D beside at most once a second whichever way it comes, and camera motion sensors are no longer searched for in the whole entity registry on every sync. The speed check now ticks a circuit monitor's readings at 8 a second (#389).
- Editor with 3D beside on: after a nudge, rotate, drop, undo or redo the changed floor is modelled in short slices before the 3D beside shows it, and its light surface and lighting are kept, so the edit no longer causes a long frame (#390).
- Editor: an edit renders the editor once, not three times: the plan the panel hands back is recognised as the editor's own, and pack updates are only asked for when a plan comes from elsewhere or the packs change, never on every drag move (#391).
- 3D view: a device sync of a large home does less: the weather and locating sensors are no longer searched for among all states, and each item's base height, room and energy role are worked out once per plan. Beside the editor, measured values alone (power, energy) show at most every 2 seconds; switches and lamps within 1 second (#393, first part).
- Editor: selecting an item is quicker on a large home: the entity lists no longer count all states on every call, and the side panel is only set back to its top when it was scrolled down, without forcing a layout (#392).
- Holiday effects are gentler and no longer run all the time: fewer, fainter pieces and a lighter edge glow, shown for 15 seconds when the view opens, then they fade out. A new house setting, Holidays > Holiday effect (off / brief / 1 minute / always), changes how long (#385).

## Home 3D 1.0.1 (5 November 2026)

- Every bundled language now has all the texts: about 1,300 texts that were missing in the 16 languages other than English and German (and some Home Assistant service and options texts) are translated, so they no longer show in English.
- Restoring a backup with a home whose plan can't be read (of a newer Home 3D, or invalid) restores the other homes; the unreadable one is skipped and named in the notice, and the homes here stay as they are (#380)
- A pet's event trigger can no longer name `*` or a very frequent core event (state_changed, call_service and the like); a stored plan with such a trigger loads with it left out, and the editor says why (#359).
- A failed load of the app (a network blip, Home Assistant still starting) is tried again under a new URL, by the start file and by the card loader, instead of failing until a manual reload; a loaded app is never loaded twice (#360).
- A slow build.json no longer loads a second copy of the app: every copy of the start file on a page loads the app URL the first one chose (#361).
- Now-playing cards group only players of one TV: on the same TV item, or close together and all TV players (or the same name); a speaker or PC on the same bench or desk keeps its own card (#362).
- The 3D view's own plan save (no panel or card around it) now sends the revision it started from and, when someone else saved first, makes the change again on their newer plan (#363).
- A home keeps at most 300 pictures (96 MB together); beyond that the editor says so, and setting or removing a picture now waits its turn with the other changes of the home (#364).
- Time travel answers the car trackers of every home, or of the home a request names (optional `home_id`), not only the active one (#371).
- The lights-to-lamps migration keeps a light's mount height as the lamp's `mount_y`, on the server and in the browser (#381).
- A drag in the editor that is still held when someone else's plan arrives (a reload, or a refused save loading their plan) ends there instead of writing the older plan back over theirs on the next move, Esc, second finger or release (#382).
- Pasting or duplicating an item as a non-admin editor drops its perform actions from the copy (with a notice), so the copy no longer locks every later save; a save refused for that reason names the item (#383).
- The 3D view's quick changes (settings, hiding a room) now count as undo steps, so opening the editor afterwards keeps the history. A picture on a screen that failed to load is tried again after a minute, and the picture cache is emptied when the home changes and kept small. A closed editor or 3D view no longer stays in memory through the back button's bookkeeping. A link on an item to another website always asks first and names the site; paths like `//host` and `/\host` are no longer accepted as paths (a stored plan with one loads with that link target left out). The media cover in the quick menu no longer breaks on addresses with spaces or brackets (#384).
- Editor drafts are kept per home and stay until restored or discarded, so a failed save on one home or a save in another tab no longer erases another draft; a draft notes the revision it was made on, and restoring it onto a newer plan says so and keeps the plan shown as a restore point first; a stored draft is offered before the automatic door/window link save; closing the page within the save pause keeps the last edit as a draft; a failed save is made again when the connection is back; revisions going backwards (a backup put back) no longer hide others' saves (#378).
- Loading a plan loses nothing: a plan with parts this version cannot read (say after going back to an older release) is shown repaired but stays on disk unchanged until an editor saves, and is kept as a restore point newer ones never push out; the clean-up at start keeps pictures set in the last 7 days and never runs against a missing or empty plan; a lost list of homes is rebuilt from the homes' stores; a damaged plan store makes its home read-only instead of empty; undoing a failed backup restore keeps a read-only home read-only (#379).
- Voice: a view that did not understand a sentence no longer beats one that does (its reply counts only when no view understands); with no Home 3D screen open Assist answers at once instead of after 6 seconds; a sentence said to a voice satellite goes only to the open screen named like the satellite's device or its area (other screens stay where they are), and a sentence for a screen reaches only the views of that name (#366).
- Voice: while people are shown to admins only, where people are (a locating answer's rooms) is sent only to the views of admins; the server filters instead of trusting each screen to hide it (#367).
- Voice sentences: only a sentence file exactly as Home 3D wrote it counts as Home 3D's; a file you trimmed (for example without "show [me] {text}") is no longer rewritten or removed. Refreshing the file when locating learns new names and switching the sentences off no longer race (one write at a time), and a waiting refresh stops when the integration unloads (#369).
- The locating room sensors write a state only when their own room or settings change, and once a minute while the device is heard; probability, since and last_seen stay in the state but are no longer recorded (#365).
- Locating after a reload of Home 3D: open views follow the new service at once, a change made just before is kept, the lab recorder stops with the entry (and its recordings are deleted when Home 3D is removed), a presence sensor moved to another area confirms its new room, and locating switched on before Bluetooth has loaded starts once it has (#368).
- Locating does far less work each second in a big house: a faster distance, the pooled samples built once per estimate and capped at 120 a room, and a window measured only against the samples of the four rooms whose middle is nearest; 4 learned devices x 12 rooms plus 5 devices on the pool went from 93 ms to about 5 ms an estimate (#370).
- Packs: no pack replaces one of another kind; only Home 3D's own GitHub account may use the `theovencook.` ids and publisher, replacing a bundled or official pack asks first naming it, an index entry installs only the pack it names, and only the default index marks packs official (#372).
- Packs: the installed release with other content or from another place asks first (also for a pinned pack) and can be rolled back; auto-update holds a release that changes the kind or publisher or removes placed items, and says why (#373).
- Packs: the pack index is read and checked outside the event loop, and the list of placed items with an update compares stored hashes before checking any copy again (#374).
- Packs: a folder import is measured before it is sent (too large instead of a dropped connection), GitHub installs keep to the same byte limit, and a full backup sends large packs ahead in parts (#375).
- Backup restore: packs go through the checks of an import (sizes, kept files, one entry per id, pins, older releases, kind, rollback), and a custom item whose id is taken by a different local item comes in renamed with the restored plans following; renamed and skipped items are listed (#376).
- Pack update checks ask GitHub outside the lock (imports, removals and roll backs no longer wait for the network), stop and back off on a rate limit, and save the round with per-pack errors when something unexpected fails (#377).

## Home 3D 0.2.1 (2 November 2026)

- A Home Assistant restart is no longer shown as motion: the camera detection pins only linger for a detection this page saw, and the motion trail skips sensors coming back from unavailable or unknown (#299).
- The clock sits behind the room panel, menus and pop-ups (#313).
- The camera wall is a free window: drag its header to move it, drag an edge or corner to resize it, double click the header to reset; the place is remembered on this device, and the Cameras button stays readable while the wall is open (#314).
- **Walking on the stairs** keeps both floors drawn, in place and at full strength, until one steps off at the top or the bottom (#312).

### Plan format version, contract and failure cases (#165, #166, #167)

- **Plans carry a real format version (now 2)** and Home Assistant brings older plans up to date when it loads them, and when a save, a backup, an imported plan or a restore point brings one in; voice, time travel and the sensors see the same plan as the editor. Lights placed as devices become lamps on the server (keeping their name, aliases, actions and marker). The plan as stored before stays as a restore point.
- **One contract for both sides (#166):** a plan with every field of the format (`frontend/src/plan-contract.fixture.ts`, copied to `tests/fixtures/contract/plan.json`) is checked against the TypeScript model and the Python schema, and the websocket commands are listed in `tests/fixtures/contract/websocket.json`, which the frontend's calls are checked against. Eight fields the editor already saved but the server did not check yet (a door's lock, a radiator's heat sensor, a lamp's only colour, the running watts, a camera look's camera, a camera's own detection sensors, the household notes list, the editor's opening floor) are now checked. `building/get` and `version` report a websocket `protocol` number.
- **A stored plan that does not validate is no longer passed on as it is:** its invalid parts are left out, named in a notice, with the plan as it was kept as a restore point. A plan beyond repair, or one saved by a newer Home 3D, is shown read-only with a notice and never written over; a restore point or a backup can still replace it.
- **Saves and deletes in failure cases (#167):** a save whose write fails leaves the plan and its revision as they are on disk (and tells no open view of a change that was not stored); deleting a home waits for a save that is running and refuses one that comes after, so no store of a deleted home is written again.
