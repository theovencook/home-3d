# Cooling checklist (#146)

Follow-up: ceiling fans from a pack are static models; their blades only turn for the built-in fan lamp (the viewer
spins blades for the `lamp_fan` type only, and a pack item's `light` cannot be `fan`). Turning pack ceiling fans is a
separate follow-up ticket.

Fans, air conditioners and evaporative coolers in `theovencook.devices/heating/`. Sizes are W x D x H in mm. Each item
is modelled by us from simple shapes; no maker pictures or 3D files are used. The existing items (`ceiling_fan_42`,
`ceiling_fan_52` and their light versions, `pedestal_fan`, `tower_fan`, `tower_fan_column`, `air_conditioner_wall`,
`air_conditioner_outdoor`, `air_conditioner_portable`) are not repeated here.

Status: `maker` = a manufacturer page, data sheet or flyer gives the figure; `retailer` = only shops give it;
`unverified` = nothing found in the search pass (done 30 October 2026), the figure is our estimate of a typical product
and should be checked against the real device. Where one model's size stands for a range (for example the 3.5 kW wall
split uses the 2.5 kW casing) the note says so. Many figures came from search summaries of the pages, not from reading
the pages in full, so they are worth one more look when an item is used for a real plan. Where listings disagree the
note says which one was used.

The `watts` of each electric item is a typical draw, not a rating. Tags such as `air conditioning`, `fan`, `climate`,
`heat pump` and the BTU or kW rating help find an item for a Home Assistant `climate` or `fan` entity. Window items and
the hose kit are `wall` items at 0.9 m (a window sill), wall splits and wall fans are `wall` items, ceiling fans and
cassettes are `ceiling` items, and desk, clip and USB fans sit on a `surface`.
