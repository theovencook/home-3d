# Gaming checklist (#148)

Consoles, controllers, handhelds and gaming-setup gear. Sizes are W x D x H in mm as the item stands in the plan
(upright and flat variants swap the long edge between width and height). We model each device ourselves from simple
shapes; no maker pictures, 3D files or logos are used. Items sit in `theovencook.living` (`media/` and `lamp/`) and
`theovencook.office` (`tech/` and `office/`). Checked 30 October 2026.

Status: `maker` = a manufacturer page gives the figure (a page that was fetched, or a maker spec page read from a
search result, as the Source column says); `retailer` = shops, reference sites or press give it (the sites are named);
`unverified` = nothing usable loaded, the figure is our estimate.

What could not be reached: xbox.com, playstation.com, meta.com and nintendo.com's Switch 1 pages gave no sizes, so
Xbox, PlayStation and Quest figures are `retailer` (several agree). Still `unverified`: PlayStation Portal and PS VR2
(sources conflict, probably packaging sizes), Switch Pro Controller, Joy-Con, NES and the two Classic Minis (only box
sizes loaded), and the generic items (gaming PCs, gaming monitors, racing cockpit, arcade cabinet, charging dock,
headset stand, RGB light bars), which are class sizes rather than one product.

Existing items left alone: `gaming_chair`, `huvudspelare_gaming_chair`, `utespelare_gaming_desk_160x80`. The plain
`monitor_27`, `monitor_34`, `pc_tower` and `pc_tower_full` already existed and are not duplicated: the gaming monitors
here are a different look (gaming foot, curved ultrawide) and the gaming PCs add RGB fans and a small form factor.

`watts` is the typical draw while playing, a round estimate. Handhelds and controllers are battery devices and are not
`electric`.
