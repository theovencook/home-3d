# IKEA UK models

Models: 200. Done: 200 (408 items).

The 200 IKEA UK models the `theovencook.ikea-uk` pack is built towards (tickets #30 to #34), so each batch picks
from this list without overlap. Most come from the IKEA UK best-seller list as it stood on 29 October 2026
(furniture, lighting and larger decor; small kitchenware, textiles, bulbs and spare parts are left out); a few are
long-standing range classics that were not on the list that day.

How a model goes into the pack (see also `docs/packs.md`, *The IKEA UK pack*):

- Read its sizes from the IKEA UK product pages, a few at a time; model it ourselves from boxes, cylinders,
  lofts, spheres, lathes and rounded boxes. Never copy IKEA's pictures or 3D files.
- `name` is a readable English name, `ikea` the IKEA product name; one item per size, layout and left/right
  version, grouped by `family`. Colourways are colour roles plus the colour presets, listed in `tags`.
- Item budget: the pack may hold at most 500 items. Batch 1 used 114 for 40 models, which leaves about 2.4 items
  per model for the other 160: keep to the sizes people buy and fold near-identical sizes into one.
- When a model is in, set its status to `done` with the number of items; if IKEA UK no longer sells it, mark it
  `dropped` and take the next one from the same room.

Best-seller rank: its place on the IKEA UK best-seller list on 29 October 2026; `-` means a range classic.
